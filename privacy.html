Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA Clipper ("GTA Clipper", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the GTA Clipper website and software (the "Service"), including when you connect a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information from connected platforms

When you connect an account, you sign in on the platform's own login page and choose what to allow. We receive only what you allow:

  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API, scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar; an access token and refresh token; your posting options (such as available privacy levels); and the status and ID of videos sent through the Service.
  • YouTube (YouTube Data API): an access token and refresh token for your channel, your channel ID, and the IDs and URLs of videos uploaded through the Service.
  • Instagram (Instagram Graph API): an access token, your Instagram account ID and username, and the IDs and URLs of Reels published through the Service.

Content you publish

The video clips, titles, captions and hashtags you choose to publish, and the source stream links you add.

Usage and performance data

For videos published through the Service, public view and like counts read back from the platform, used to show you how your clips perform.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your platform passwords, payment information, contacts, messages, location, or any information about people who watch your videos. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

2. How we use information

  • To upload and publish the clips you approve to the accounts you connected.
  • To send TikTok clips to your TikTok inbox as drafts, or post them with the settings you choose.
  • To show you the status, links and performance of your published clips.
  • To keep the Service secure, fix errors and comply with law.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or selling, and we do not use content from connected platforms to train machine-learning models.

3. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only:

  • With the platform you publish to, when you publish: the video, title and caption go to TikTok, YouTube or Instagram through their official APIs.
  • To comply with law, if required by a valid legal request, or to protect the rights and safety of users and others.

4. Third-party services

Your use of each connected platform is also governed by that platform's own terms and privacy policy:

  • TikTok: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • YouTube: YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy
  • Instagram: Instagram Privacy Policy

5. Storage and security

Access tokens and account identifiers are stored on the device that runs GTA Clipper, in files readable only by the operator's user account, and are sent only to the platform that issued them over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Clips are stored on that device until they are published or deleted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Retention

Tokens are kept while your account stays connected and are deleted when you disconnect or ask us to delete them. Clip files and publishing records are kept only as long as needed to publish and report on your clips, and are deleted on request.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect: revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions), in your Google Account (Third-party connections), or in Instagram (Settings, Apps and websites). The Service can no longer access the account after you revoke it.
  • Access and deletion: you can ask what information we hold about you and ask us to delete it. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or port your information and to object to processing. Contact us to use them. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has used the Service, contact us and we will delete the information.

9. International transfers

The Service is operated from the United States. If you use it from another country, your information is processed in the United States and by the platforms you connect, which may have different data-protection laws.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will notify connected users before the change takes effect.

11. Contact
