Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

GTA Clipper ("the app") is a personal publishing tool. It is used only by its owner to post videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What data the app accesses

  • OAuth access tokens for the owner's YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, granted by the owner through each platform's official sign-in.
  • Basic account info returned by those platforms (such as account ID and username), used only to publish to the right account.

How the data is used

Tokens are used only to upload videos and captions that the owner chose to publish, and to read back the post link. The app does not collect data about viewers or any other people.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deleting data

Access can be removed at any time in each platform's connected-apps settings (Google Account permissions, Instagram/Meta "Apps and websites", TikTok "Manage app permissions"). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
