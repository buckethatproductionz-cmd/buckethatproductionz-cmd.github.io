GTA Clipper

GTA Clipper is a personal tool run by the owner of the Best Streamer Clips accounts. It cuts short vertical highlight clips from gaming livestreams and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

It is not a public service: only the owner's own accounts are connected, and no other users sign in to it.
